## Changes

- **Express mock option types follow the real types** (#515).
  `ExpressHandlerOptions` and `ExpressStreamHandlerOptions` derive from
  `@jaypie/express`, so `chaos`, `fabric`, `logBody`, `name`, `scrub`,
  `secrets`, `sensitiveHeaders`, `unavailable`, `contentType`, and `format` are
  typed. `locals`, `setup`, `teardown`, and `validate` keep their mock shapes.
- The import is type-only; `./mock` still loads without `@jaypie/express`
  installed.
