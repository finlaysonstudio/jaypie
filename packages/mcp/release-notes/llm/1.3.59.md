## Changes

### One corrective turn (issue #610)

A `format` request answered with prose or with nothing took the corrective turn (only the `structured_output` tool offered) on every turn until `turns` ran out. With the default budget of 24, an empty Gemini reply cost 24 requests, each resending the full prompt, before the chain failed over.

- The corrective turn is taken at most once per run. A second miss settles: empty content fails over as in 1.3.58, and prose completes as it did when turns ran out.
- Applies to every adapter with a corrective turn: Fireworks, Google, and Mistral.

## Testing

- `OperateLoop` specs cover one corrective turn with the default budget for empty content and for prose.
