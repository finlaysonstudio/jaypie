## Changes

- `skill("llm")` documents Mistral: the provider/match-word table, the
  `MODEL.MISTRAL_*` catalog entries, `MISTRAL_API_KEY`, the binary
  `reasoning_effort` mapping, and the routing precedence that keeps
  `mistral.mistral-*` on Bedrock and `mistralai/*` on OpenRouter
- `skill("llm")` documents streamed exchange capture: what `stream()` settles,
  how an abandoned generator is recorded, and why there is no
  `response.exchange` on a generator
- `skill("llm")` gains a Cancellation section covering the `signal` option,
  `LlmAbortError`, and teardown abort on an abandoned stream
