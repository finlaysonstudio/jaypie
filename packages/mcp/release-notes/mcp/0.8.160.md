## Changes

- **`skill("JaypieDatadogForwarder")`** gains an "Invoke Permissions" section
  covering token-ARN imports and the events-rule warning acknowledgement.
- **`skill("cdk")`** gains "Origin Access Control" and "Importing a Lambda by
  Token ARN" sections, and the WAF summary lists the new config keys.
- **`skill("waf")`** documents the hardened log bucket, `waf.logRetention`, and
  the merge semantics of `redactedHeaders` / `redactedFields`.
- **`skill("web")`** gains an "Origin Access Control" section and documents
  `logRetention` and the hardened log buckets.
- **`skill("migrations")`** documents `logGroup`, `logRetention`, and
  `reservedConcurrentExecutions`, including the inert-`logRetention` gap
  deferred to 2.0 (#568) and the zero-concurrency throw.
